# Changelog

## 0.8.0 - 2026-10-13
- **Hold R + drag to rotate the canvas** (scrubby rotate), like scrubby zoom.
- Configurable rotate_key and rotate_sensitivity; Z/Zoom and R/Rotate both
  settable in the Settings dialog or magmarita.json.
- Clears the canvas-input "Select Layer" binding that used R.

## 0.7.1 - 2026-10-13
- Fixed H/U/L/I/Shift+N not working: unbinds the Krita defaults that claimed
  them, and enforces the keymap on every start.

## 0.6.1 - 2026-10-13
