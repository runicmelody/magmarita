# MagmaRita branding

## The mark

A rounded-square badge in **Krita's palette** (blue to teal gradient) with a white
**M** whose inner valley and top droplet nod to Magma's volcano/ink-drop feel.

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Krita blue | `#2D9EDE` | gradient start |
| Krita teal | `#17BFAE` | gradient end |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | the M mark |
| Ink | `#3A4A5A` | taglines |

## Usage rules

- Keep the badge square; don't stretch it.
- Leave clear space of at least 25% of the badge width.
- Use the white mark on the gradient, or the gradient badge on white/dark.
- Don't add effects, outlines or rotate the mark.
