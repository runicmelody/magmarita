MagmaRita Scrubby Zoom

Hold the shortcut (Z by default) and drag horizontally to zoom relative to the current zoom level.

  • The zoom anchor is taken from Krita's real zoom value, so it starts from where you are instead of jumping.
  • The last zoom is remembered per document.
  • Sensitivity is configurable (pixels per doubling) in Tools > Scripts > MagmaRita > Settings....

Rotate

Hold the rotate key (R by default) and drag horizontally to rotate the canvas. Drag left/right to spin it in that direction.

Both keys and their sensitivity are configurable in Tools > Scripts > MagmaRita > Settings... (zoom_key / rotate_key, zoom_sensitivity / rotate_sensitivity).
